## Design engineering and systems questions

### What is Naveen’s approach to design systems?
The portfolio shows work on reusable components, shared typography tokens, documentation, ownership, and connecting Figma variables to CSS. [Design × Engineering](case-study.html?slug=design-engineering) · [Typography](case-study.html?slug=typography-system)

### What lifecycle did he define for shared components?
The six stages are Define, Align, Implement, Capture, Share, and Evolve. The case study also describes these as design, review, engineering, documentation, release, and maintenance. [Design × Engineering](case-study.html?slug=design-engineering)

### What does “Dev SPOC” mean in his case study?
It means a clear engineering-side Single Point of Contact for component questions, implementation decisions, and updates. The case study says this was intended to clarify ownership without adding another process layer. [Design × Engineering](case-study.html?slug=design-engineering)

### How did he connect design tokens to code?
In the typography project, he created a minimal type scale using Figma variables and mapped tokens to CSS custom properties and classes, working with developers on the handoff. [Typography](case-study.html?slug=typography-system)

### How did he use AI in design-system work?
He describes designing reusable Claude Skills and workflows for drafting documentation, transforming design-system information, checking consistency, and reducing repetitive work. He says design decisions and validation remained with him. [Design × Engineering](case-study.html?slug=design-engineering)

## Project questions

### What is DesignOS?
DesignOS is an independent, interactive concept for an AI-native design workspace. It connects design systems, research, product decisions, and team knowledge; it is not described as a shipped product. [DesignOS case study](case-study.html?slug=designos) · [Interactive concept](https://www.mrnk.in/designos/)

### What problem does DesignOS explore?
Design knowledge is spread across tools, so context can be lost when a team makes a design decision. DesignOS explores making that context available in the workflow. [DesignOS](case-study.html?slug=designos)

### What did Naveen contribute to DesignOS?
The case study lists concept, product, and interaction design for a fully interactive product experience. It describes the project team as independent and solo. [DesignOS](case-study.html?slug=designos)

### What is the Design × Engineering Collaboration project?
It is an internal initiative to make shared component work more predictable through a six-stage lifecycle, clearer ownership, lightweight documentation, and reusable AI Skills. [Design × Engineering](case-study.html?slug=design-engineering)

### What did Naveen do on the teacher experience redesign?
He was the sole product designer for a redesign of the teacher application, including onboarding, dashboard, class view, syllabus, student profiles, and supporting workflows. [Teacher Experience](case-study.html?slug=teacher-experience)

### What changed in the teacher experience?
The redesign made the current class and performance information easier to scan, surfaced AI recommendations earlier, added a dedicated Hurrey Konnect screen, and reorganized supporting teacher workflows. [Teacher Experience](case-study.html?slug=teacher-experience)

### How was the teacher redesign validated?
Naveen interviewed teachers about the new AI and application functionality and asked for feedback on old and new versions. The case study reports clearer functionality and navigation, and says the redesign passed WCAG 2.0 compliance. It does not state the number of teachers interviewed. [Teacher Experience](case-study.html?slug=teacher-experience)

### What is the School Admissions Platform?
It is a school administration product designed to support the months-long admissions journey for administrators and parents. Naveen was the sole product designer from problem framing through the final experience. [School Admissions](case-study.html?slug=school-admissions)

### What research did he do for school admissions?
He interviewed school staff and principals and studied 20 schools’ online and offline application forms. The case study does not state the number of interview participants. [School Admissions](case-study.html?slug=school-admissions)

### What did the admissions experience include?
It included class-wise application forms, prebuilt and custom fields, shareable links and QR codes, application tracking, digital status updates, and dashboard insights such as Potential Around You and Popularity Score. [School Admissions](case-study.html?slug=school-admissions)

### What is the Scalable Typography System?
It is an internal design-system initiative that reduced 25+ typography styles to a 10-style token scale, connecting Figma variables with CSS custom properties. [Typography System](case-study.html?slug=typography-system)

### What results does the typography case study list?
It lists 25+ styles reduced to 10, 100% of design and development working from the same values, and 50% faster implementation. These are portfolio-reported project results. [Typography System](case-study.html?slug=typography-system)

### What is Revenue Optimization Insights?
It is a solo analytics dashboard concept for merchants, designed to connect revenue overview, possible causes of loss, comparisons, and recommendations in a decision-oriented view. [Revenue Optimization](case-study.html?slug=revenue-optimization)

### What is One Home for Store Employees?
It is a self-initiated Android concept that brings Receiving, Stowing, and Inventory Audit into one employee experience. The portfolio says it is a concept and functional prototype, not a launched More Retail product. [One Home](case-study.html?slug=more-retail) · [Interactive prototype](https://b1f899ejuz70-d.space-z.ai/app/index.html)

### What research supports One Home?
The case study describes secondary research and an informal conversation with Naveen’s uncle, who runs a small supermarket. It explicitly says this was not research with More Retail employees or a representative view of stores. [One Home](case-study.html?slug=more-retail)

### What assumptions in One Home still need validation?
The case study says the two-active-task limit, priority rules, scanning and manual lookup, quantity checks, duplicate handling, offline sync, and edge-network idea need validation with store teams. [One Home](case-study.html?slug=more-retail)

### How does the One Home concept handle receiving and inventory issues?
It guides the associate through checking a delivery one product at a time, reviewing quantities, and reporting mismatches. The case study says original entries should remain visible for comparison and supervisor approval; these are proposed design behaviors, not confirmed store policy. [One Home](case-study.html?slug=more-retail)
