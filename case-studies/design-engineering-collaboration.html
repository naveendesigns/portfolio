02 · INTEGRATE · DESIGN OPS · SYSTEMS

Design × Engineering
Collaboration.

Building a more scalable way for Design and Pixel Engineering to move from a design decision to a production-ready change.

ROLEProduct Designer
MODELTwo-track routing
SCOPE10+ product areas
DESIGN×ENGINEERING

01 / CONTEXT

The handoff was not the problem. The routing was.

Not every design change needs the same level of collaboration. Small UI changes can move quickly, while platform-wide or technically complex changes need deeper alignment.

The opportunity was to create a shared model that made the right level of collaboration obvious before work reached implementation.

02 / THE MODEL

Two tracks. One shared language.

  • Introduced a lightweight track for simple, isolated changes.
  • Created a deeper track for complex and platform-wide changes.
  • Defined what information each track needs before engineering starts.
  • Made the collaboration model easier to repeat across product areas.

03 / PROTOTYPING SYSTEM

I also built the tools needed to communicate behaviour.

Instead of treating prototypes as one-off presentation pieces, I built a reusable prototyping system that could be applied across roughly 10 major product areas.

The system made complex interactions easier to demonstrate, discuss and validate with engineering.

04 / IMPACT

Better collaboration starts before the handoff.

The model reduced ambiguity around how a request should move through Design and Pixel Engineering. It also created a more consistent way to communicate interaction details and edge cases.

05 / REFLECTION

Design operations is product design when it changes how the product gets made.

This work reinforced that a designer can improve the product not only through interfaces, but also through the systems that connect people, decisions and implementation.
