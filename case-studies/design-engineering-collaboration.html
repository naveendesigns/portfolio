Creating a clearer collaboration model between Design and Pixel Engineering so simple changes stay fast and complex changes get the right level of attention.

RoleProduct Designer
ScopeDesign operations
Reach10+ product areas

The challenge

As the product grew, design changes ranged from small visual adjustments to platform-wide changes with dependencies across multiple product areas. Treating both types of work the same created unnecessary handoff overhead and made complex work harder to coordinate.

The model

I introduced a two-track routing model that separated simple changes from complex or platform-wide changes. The intent was not to add process for its own sake, but to match the collaboration effort to the risk and scope of the change.

  • Simple changes could move through a lightweight path with clear implementation details.
  • Complex changes received deeper mapping, prototyping and cross-functional alignment.
  • Reusable patterns reduced repeated design and engineering decisions.
  • Shared prototypes helped communicate interaction details before implementation.

Prototyping at scale

I built a reusable prototyping system across 10 major product areas. This made prototypes faster to create and easier for Engineering to interpret, while preserving the flexibility needed for product-specific workflows.

The goal was not more process. It was better signal at the right moment.

Outcome

The collaboration model created a clearer path from design intent to implementation. It helped the team spend less time treating every request as a special case and more time focusing deeply where product complexity actually required it.
