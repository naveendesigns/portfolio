01 / CONTEXT

One app had to support an entire teaching day.

The teacher experience brought together classroom activity, attendance, performance, timetable information, notes, homework and connected classroom tools. The existing structure made it difficult to understand what mattered now versus what could wait.

02 / REFRAMING THE EXPERIENCE

Start with the class. Then reveal the detail.

  • Created a clearer class-level information architecture.
  • Introduced a current-class view so teachers could orient themselves quickly.
  • Surfaced AI recommendations and performance signals at useful moments.
  • Redesigned Hurrey Konnect with device status, whiteboard and file interactions.
  • Created reusable universal cards for consistent information presentation.
  • Reworked student profiles, syllabus, timetable, leave and combined attendance and events.

03 / RESEARCH + TESTING

The interface should help teachers act, not decode.

I conducted user interviews and testing throughout the redesign. Teachers found the new version easier to navigate and understand compared with the previous experience.

04 / RESULT

A more coherent teacher workflow.

The redesign brought AI recommendations into context, established reusable product patterns and created a clearer foundation for future teacher-facing features. The work also passed WCAG 2.0 compliance.
