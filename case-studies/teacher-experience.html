Redesigning the
Teacher Experience.

A ground-up redesign of the teacher application to make everyday classroom workflows easier to navigate and understand.

RoleSole Product Designer
FocusUX + IA + AI
StandardWCAG 2.0

Context

The teacher app brought together classroom activity, attendance, performance, timetable information, notes, homework and connected classroom tools. The existing structure made it difficult to understand what mattered now versus what could wait.

Reframing the experience

  • Created a clearer class-level information architecture.
  • Introduced a dynamic current-class view so teachers could quickly orient themselves.
  • Surfaced AI recommendations and performance signals at useful moments.
  • Redesigned the Hurrey Konnect area with device status, whiteboard and file interactions.
  • Created reusable universal cards for consistent information presentation.
  • Reworked student profiles, syllabus, timetable, leave and combined attendance and events.

Designing with teachers

The interface should help teachers understand the next useful action, not make them decode the system.

I conducted user interviews and testing throughout the redesign. Teachers found the new version easier to navigate and understand compared with the previous experience.

Result

The redesigned application created a more coherent teacher workflow, brought AI recommendations into context and established reusable patterns for future product work. The project also passed WCAG 2.0 compliance.
