01 / THE PROBLEM

Admissions is a workflow, not a form.

School admissions can run for three to four months. After applications arrive, administration teams face a surge of communication and need to track applications, shortlist candidates, collect payments and keep parents informed.

I focused on newly commenced, tech-savvy and affordable private schools that needed a more efficient workflow.

02 / RESEARCH

Understand the operational reality first.

  • Mapped the end-to-end school administration journey.
  • Interviewed school staff and principals.
  • Reviewed Uniapply, Ezyschooling, Edustoke and Yelloslate.
  • Studied patterns across 20 school applications.

03 / PRODUCT DIRECTION

Reduce setup. Make the queue understandable.

Onboarding was reduced from 10 questions to 6 through a step-by-step flow. The dashboard organized received, shortlisted, rejected and paid applications so administrators could understand the state of the queue at a glance.

I also designed a class-wise form builder with reusable student, parent, previous-school and document fields, plus custom fields, share links and QR codes.

04 / ACTIONABLE INFORMATION

Give schools a reason to act.

Features such as Potential Around You and Popularity Score helped schools understand opportunities around them. Filters and conditions made it easier to work through larger application sets.

05 / REFLECTION

Good product design includes the business model and the data behind it.

Working with the founder, web developer, data scientist and design interns pushed me beyond interface design into technical and business thinking.
