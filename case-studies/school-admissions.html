Hurrey · Product Design · Admissions

Making School
Admissions Easier.

Designing an administration experience that helps schools manage a months-long admissions process with less manual tracking and clearer communication.

RoleSole Product Designer
UsersSchools + parents
Research20 applications studied

The problem

School admissions can run for three to four months. After applications open, administration teams face a surge of communication and need to track applications, shortlist candidates, collect payments and keep parents informed.

I focused on newly commenced, tech-savvy and affordable private schools that needed a more efficient workflow.

From journey to product

  • Mapped the end-to-end school administration journey.
  • Interviewed school staff and principals to understand operational pain points.
  • Studied competing products including Uniapply, Ezyschooling, Edustoke and Yelloslate.
  • Reduced onboarding from 10 questions to 6 through a step-by-step flow.
  • Designed dashboards for received, shortlisted, rejected and paid applications.
  • Created a form builder with reusable fields based on patterns across 20 school applications.
  • Added share links and QR codes to make admission forms easier to distribute.

Making information actionable

The dashboard became a place to make decisions, not just a place to see numbers.

Features such as potential around you and popularity score helped schools understand their position and act on opportunities. Application filters and conditions made it easier to manage large volumes of candidates.

What changed for me

This project pushed me beyond interface design into technical and business thinking. Working with the founder, web developer, data scientist and design interns helped me understand how product decisions connect to the underlying business model and data.
