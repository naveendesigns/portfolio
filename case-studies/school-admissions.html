01 / Context

Admissions is a months-long operational journey.

School admissions typically last three to four months. The primary user is the school administration, while parents are the intermediary users.

My goal was to make the admission process easier and more efficient while improving functionality and user experience. I was the sole designer from start to finish.

02 / User journey

Start with the journey, not the interface.

I mapped the user journey to understand pain points, opportunities and emotions throughout the admission process.

  • Select an employee or assign development work.
  • Use the existing application form or create a new one.
  • Promote the school or create an admission banner.
  • Communicate with parents for enquiries.
  • Receive applications from parents.
  • Evaluate application forms.
  • Update application status to parents.

03 / Research

Understand the operational reality behind admissions.

I interviewed school staff and school principals to understand how the process actually works.

  • Schools typically assign staff to handle admissions.
  • Schools struggle to keep track of applications issued.
  • The admission cycle typically lasts 3–4 months.
  • Schools often hire advertising agencies or handle marketing themselves.
  • After parents submit forms, calls increase as parents ask for updates.
  • Parents look for education, discipline and etiquette, student results, sports and other activities.
→The admission queue revealed an important human and business context: schools sometimes use queues to communicate brand value.

04 / Competitive analysis

Find the pattern, then find the opportunity.

I reviewed existing patterns across:

  • Uniapply.com
  • Ezyschooling.com
  • Edustoke.com
  • Yelloslate.com

These platforms had similar functionality and higher average monthly traffic, but they focused on a limited set of branded schools or specific areas.

05 / Product direction

Make business intelligence part of the product.

In a brainstorming session with the founder, developer and data scientist, we identified new features that could make the product stand out and provide useful business intelligence.

Potential Around You

Helps schools estimate the number of students they can target in an area.

Popularity Score

An approximate score based on the school's popularity in its area.

QR generation

After creating an application form, schools receive a QR code to share it effortlessly.

07 / Onboarding

Reduce the questions without reducing the context.

Onboarding had many questions to ask the end user, but the experience should not feel boring.

  • Initially there were 10 questions.
  • We reduced them to 6.
  • We explored list versus step-by-step layouts.
  • We chose step-by-step because the information was crucial and would reflect in the parent portal.

08 / Dashboard

Turn a content-heavy dashboard into an action surface.

The dashboard shows applications received, shortlisted, rejected and application-fee payments. Because this is a content-heavy page with high cognitive load, information needed the right level of emphasis.

The problem

  • Difficulty tracking applications issued to parents.
  • Difficulty understanding how many parents were aware of the school.
  • Difficulty targeting the right parents, reducing applications sold.

The solution

  • Potential Around You is accessible directly from the dashboard.
  • Popularity Score is calculated from profile views and the number of views is visible.
  • Schools can copy a link or QR code and share it with parents directly.

09 / Create form

Turn a lengthy paper process into a reusable form system.

Schools can create class-wise application forms with an admission fee, opening date, closing date, extended date and allocated seats.

The problem

Physical forms required discussions with administration, creation of labels and fields, government requirements, hiring a typist, printing and issuing forms only to parents visiting the school.

The solution

I researched Google Forms, Typeform and Nopaperforms, then studied 20 different schools' online and offline applications. I created pre-built fields categorized into student, parent, previous school and document details.

Pre-built fields

Select commonly required fields instead of creating everything from scratch.

Custom fields

Add additional fields when the school needs something specific.

Share + scale

Generate a link or QR code and share the application with anyone.

10 / Manage applications

Make a growing application queue manageable.

Schools can manage all applications received from parents, filter them and accept, shortlist or reject applications after viewing student details.

The problem

  • Piles of applications are difficult to maintain.
  • Parents need updates throughout the process.
  • Manual student selection takes time.

The solution

  • Every application receives a unique application number.
  • Digital status changes directly reflect in the parent portal, reducing communication issues.
  • Field-based filters allow schools to create conditions and select the right students.
