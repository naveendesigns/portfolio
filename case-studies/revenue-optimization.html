A high-fidelity dashboard concept designed to help merchants identify revenue losses, understand root causes and act on opportunities.

UserSarah · E-commerce
FocusAnalytics + decisions
OutputHigh-fidelity prototype

The user story

Sarah runs a small e-commerce business. She can see that revenue moved, but understanding why it changed and what to do next requires pulling together multiple signals.

The dashboard needed to move beyond reporting and help merchants identify loss, compare periods and improve their strategy.

Exploring filters

  • Explored three approaches for handling time and metric filters.
  • Evaluated how combined controls affected scanning and cognitive load.
  • Selected independent core metric filters so users could change one dimension without decoding a complex control.
  • Designed a desktop high-fidelity dashboard around revenue, loss signals and comparison.

Design principle

Analytics become valuable when the next decision is obvious.

The direction focused on making revenue movement easy to scan while giving users enough context to understand the root cause behind a change.

Outcome

The final concept established a clear structure for monitoring revenue, identifying losses, comparing periods and moving toward actionable recommendations rather than passive reporting.
