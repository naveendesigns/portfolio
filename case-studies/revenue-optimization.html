01 / THE USER STORY

Reporting is not the same as understanding.

Sarah runs a small e-commerce business. She can see that revenue moved, but understanding why it changed and what to do next requires pulling together multiple signals.

The dashboard needed to move beyond reporting and help merchants identify loss, compare periods and improve their strategy.

02 / EXPLORATION

I explored the filter before committing to the dashboard.

  • Reviewed Mobbin, Dribbble, Behance and competitor analytics patterns.
  • Explored three approaches for time and metric filters.
  • Evaluated how combined controls affected scanning and cognitive load.
  • Selected independent core metric filters so users could change one dimension without decoding a complex control.

03 / FINAL DIRECTION

Make revenue movement easy to scan.

The high-fidelity desktop concept brings revenue, loss signals and comparison into one structured view. The visual hierarchy is designed to help a merchant move from what changed to why it changed and what might be worth doing next.

04 / REFLECTION

Analytics become valuable when the next decision is obvious.

The project helped me think about analytics as a decision-making experience rather than a collection of charts.
