Linked Campaign
Propagation.

Turning a technically complex relationship between campaigns into a clear, predictable product experience.

RoleProduct Designer
FocusWorkflow design
ScopeEnd to end

The problem

Linked campaigns introduced dependencies that were not obvious from the interface. Users needed to understand what changed, what would propagate and where the source of truth lived.

The challenge was larger than a screen redesign. I needed to understand the backend behaviour, existing product rules and the points where the current experience diverged from the intended model.

How I approached it

  • Mapped the workflow end to end across connected campaign states.
  • Investigated backend behaviour to understand propagation logic.
  • Identified a production bug and a mismatch between the product behaviour and design.
  • Created a phased product plan instead of forcing every dependency into one release.
  • Built an interactive prototype to validate the experience and communicate edge cases with Product and Engineering.

Design principle

Make relationships visible before users need to reason about them.

The final direction focused on clearer hierarchy, predictable states and progressive disclosure, so the system could remain powerful without making every dependency visible at once.

What I learned

Complex SaaS problems often require product thinking before interaction design. The strongest design decisions came from understanding the system underneath the UI and creating a shared model that Product, Design and Engineering could use.
