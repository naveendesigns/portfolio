Your next design decision needs context from all of it — and gets none of it.

Designers spend time finding context instead of using it. The knowledge exists. It’s just never in the room when the decision is made.

01

Fragmented knowledge

The reasoning behind important decisions lives across tools that don’t talk to each other — so every project starts with archaeology.

02

Repeated questions

“Do we already have a pattern for this?” gets asked in Slack every week — and answered differently every time.

03

Lost context

Six months after a project ships, nobody remembers why the design is the way it is. Including the people who designed it.

The insight

The problem isn’t a lack of documentation. It’s a lack of context at the moment a decision is made.

DesignOS turns existing knowledge into an active design capability.

How DesignOS works

From scattered knowledge to working context.

01

Connect

Connect the tools where your design knowledge already lives. No migration, no re-documentation.

02

Understand

DesignOS maps relationships between components, decisions, research and guidelines — a living graph of how your product thinks.

03

Assist

While designers work, the system offers contextual recommendations — always with evidence and confidence attached.

04

Learn

Approved decisions and new patterns feed back into the knowledge base. The system gets sharper with every decision.

Human control

AI recommends. Designers decide.

01

Recommendation

The AI proposes a possible decision — and never silently applies one.

02

Evidence

Every proposal shows where it came from — sources, linked and inspectable.

03

Confidence

Uncertainty is stated, not hidden. Low confidence means “check this yourself”.

04

Human decision

Accept, modify, or reject. The call — and the record — stays with a person.
